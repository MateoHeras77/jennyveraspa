Balance, not transformation. **Facial harmonization in Cuenca** at Jenny Vera Spa uses hyaluronic acid fillers to correct disproportions, restore lost volume and define contours, always after a medical aesthetic assessment and with a conservative principle: the result should look like you, well rested.

## What facial harmonization is

It is a set of procedures using hyaluronic acid, a substance the body produces naturally and that is gradually reabsorbed. Unlike Botox, which relaxes muscles, fillers **add volume and structure**: they fill a fold, project a chin, define a contour or hydrate the lips from within.

It is not about placing product in many areas. It is about identifying the two or three points that throw the face off balance and treating only those.

## Areas we treat

- **Lips**: hydration, border definition and correction of asymmetries.
- **Tear trough**: improves the look of hollow under-eyes and a tired gaze.
- **Nasolabial folds**: softens the lines between nose and mouth.
- **Chin and jawline**: adds projection and defines the profile.
- **Non-surgical nose reshaping**: corrects the nasal profile without surgery.

## Benefits

- Balances facial proportions while respecting your features.
- Restores volume in the areas that lose it with age.
- Visible results from the first session, and reversible.
- Combines well with Botox, biostimulation and skin quality treatments.

## Our approach: conservative, in two stages

We would rather do too little and adjust afterwards. In the first session we place a moderate amount, bring you back after two weeks — once the product has settled and swelling has gone down — and only then decide whether more is needed.

This is a medical aesthetic procedure: it requires individual facial assessment, traceable product and precise technique. During your assessment we review your medical history, rule out contraindications and explain which result is realistic in your case and which is not.

If you are considering a blepharoplasty, two questions come before all the others: what it costs and how long recovery takes. Both answers depend on your case, but knowing the usual ranges in Ecuador and what recovery really looks like helps you decide with information and without surprises. This guide covers indicative prices, what pushes them up or down, week-by-week recovery and the non-surgical alternatives available if you are not ready for an operating room.

## What blepharoplasty is

Blepharoplasty is surgery that rejuvenates the eye area by removing or redistributing excess skin, muscle or fat in the eyelids. There are two main types:

- **Upper blepharoplasty:** corrects drooping lids and that "heavy" or tired look. In some cases it also improves the visual field when excess skin covers part of the eye.
- **Lower blepharoplasty:** treats under-eye bags and sagging, reshaping the lower contour.

They can be performed separately or in the same session (bilateral), depending on what your face needs.

## Blepharoplasty prices in Ecuador

Most clinics in Ecuador do not publish a fixed rate: the figure is set after an in-person assessment, because every eyelid and every goal is different. Even so, based on public references from clinics and surgeons in the region, these are the indicative ranges usually quoted:

| Procedure | Indicative range (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Upper blepharoplasty | $800 – $1,800 |
| Lower blepharoplasty | $1,000 – $2,000 |
| Bilateral (upper + lower) | $1,800 – $3,500 |

## What affects the price

Two quotes can differ a great deal for legitimate reasons. The factors that weigh most are:

- **Type of blepharoplasty:** upper is usually less expensive than lower or bilateral.
- **Complexity of the case:** significant excess skin, large bags or a functional component (reduced visual field) call for a more advanced technique.
- **The surgeon's experience and reputation:** a specialist with a proven track record charges for that expertise, and it reduces risk.
- **Anaesthesia and theatre:** local anaesthesia with sedation or general, plus use of an accredited clinic, is part of the total.
- **Tests and follow-ups:** pre-surgical tests and check-up appointments may or may not be included in the package.

When comparing quotes, check what each one covers: the cheapest is not always the one that includes everything.

## Week-by-week recovery

Recovering from a blepharoplasty is more manageable than many people imagine, but it takes patience and care. This is the usual progression.

### Days 1 to 3

Rest, cold compresses at intervals and sleeping with your head elevated. Swelling and bruising around the eyes are normal. You take the prescribed medication for pain and inflammation.

### Weeks 1 to 2

Swelling drops noticeably and many people resume light activities and even work between days 7 and 10. Stitches are usually removed and the scars begin to blend in. Careful makeup is generally allowed after the second week.

### Weeks 3 to 6

You return to normal activity and exercise progressively. The eyes already look rested, though the result keeps refining.

### Months 3 to 6

The scars, placed in the natural folds of the eyelid, fade almost completely and the final result becomes clear.
